# Account (/system/account)

Your admin account is the login for Pulsarr itself, separate from your Plex account. Open **Account settings** from the account menu at the bottom of the sidebar.

- **Profile** has the **Email** and **Username** you sign in with.
- **Password** takes a **New password** and **Confirm new password**. Leave both blank to keep your current one.

Enter your **Current password** under **Confirm changes** to save any of them.

To turn sign-in off, or skip it on your local network, set `authenticationMethod` in [`.env`](/installation/environment-variables#security).
